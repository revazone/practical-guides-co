Questions, answered

The short version of everything people ask before their first kit.

What is a Drop-In AI Kit?

A portable PDF that carries its own instructions for an AI chat. You drop the file into the chat you already use, and it interviews you, fills in its forms, and hands back a finished document you can print and sign.

Which AI chats does it work with?

ChatGPT, Muse, Gemini, and similar chats all work. A free account is enough.

Do I need an AI chat?

No. Every kit also works the plain way: print it and fill it in by hand. The AI simply does the typing and the organizing.

Is this legal advice?

No. The kits are general information and organization tools. For legal questions about your situation, consult a licensed professional.

What do I get after buying?

An instant PDF download from Payhip. Drop it into your AI chat, or print it and start writing.

What is the refund promise?

If a kit is not right for your situation, email us within 30 days of purchase and we will refund you.

Does the kit send my information anywhere?

No. The PDF runs inside your own AI chat. The kit itself collects nothing and phones nothing home. One caution: never give any AI a Social Security number, a bank login, or a full card number.

What does patent pending mean?

The AI-Ready system inside these kits is the subject of U.S. Provisional Application No. 64/187,478, filed October 29, 2026. Patent pending means that application is on file while the system is examined.
